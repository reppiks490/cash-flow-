# HVAC Revenue Recovery — Pilot Offer v0.1 (unvalidated)

**Status: proposed commercial experiment, not a live offer, customer commitment, or proven ROI.** Owner approval and legal/compliance review are required before any outreach or live use.

## Problem hypothesis

Small HVAC contractors may lose revenue when incoming service inquiries and open estimates receive delayed or inconsistent follow-up. Validate the magnitude and workflow with owner interviews; do not claim a specific lost-revenue rate without source data.

## Narrow value proposition

"See which inbound HVAC inquiries and outstanding quotes need attention, and prepare reviewable next-step follow-ups without replacing your current CRM."

## Proposed 14-day controlled pilot

1. Discovery call and process mapping with owner approval; identify the existing lead intake and CRM source.
2. Import **only approved, permissioned** records into private, tenant-isolated infrastructure after security and data-processing review. Until then, synthetic demo only.
3. Deduplicate and categorize stale inquiries and estimates; create **drafts** for staff approval, never auto-send.
4. Staff handles actual communication using approved provider workflow, with opt-outs and suppression enforced.
5. Review a weekly report: intake, staff-approved follow-ups, actual sends (if independently verified), confirmed appointments, confirmed closed jobs, and revenue attribution where authenticated receipts exist.
6. Provide an exportable summary and delete/return data at termination under agreed retention terms.

**Explicit exclusions:** no promised recovered revenue, autonomous calls/SMS/email, advertising, paid lead generation, automatic quoting, guaranteed appointments, CRM replacement, regulated financial advice, or sharing customer information with unapproved AI vendors.

## Pricing hypotheses (not validated)

- Proposed recurring service + software: **$700/month per customer**, month-to-month after a trial/pilot agreement.
- A no-cost or fixed-fee 14-day pilot may reduce purchase friction but incurs operator labor. Decide after real discovery; do not advertise either until approved.
- Five $700 subscriptions yield $3,500 gross MRR; achieving $2,500/month pre-tax net requires all monthly operating expenses (including attributable labor) <= $1,000. See UNIT_ECONOMICS.md.
- No payment processing or customer onboarding is currently active.

## Pilot acceptance criteria

A pilot is eligible only after: owner authorization; privacy/security controls; legitimate consent and permitted outreach basis; CRM/vendor terms; verified suppression; tenant isolation; incident response; a written scope/termination policy; approved billing/payment; and QA sign-off.

**Product acceptance:** leads are deduplicated; source provenance preserved; each proposed draft requires approval; no external side effects in synthetic mode; opt-out blocks follow-up; appointment and job outcomes are distinguished; cash is only counted after verified receipt. No single lead can be double-counted as multiple paid jobs.

## Discovery interview guide (do not contact prospects yet)

Ask 10–15 qualified HVAC operators, with permission, about (1) weekly inbound inquiry volume by channel, (2) where leads are recorded, (3) time to first response, (4) frequency and reasons estimates remain open, (5) current CRM and dispatch tooling, (6) who owns follow-up, (7) typical closed-job contribution margin, (8) existing consent/opt-out procedures, (9) who controls purchasing and the procurement process, (10) willingness to pay for an auditable 14-day pilot. Do not solicit personal customer details or access credentials.

**Validation threshold (internal experiment, not an observed result):** interview at least 10 operators; require at least 3 to request a scoped pilot and at least 1 to accept written commercial terms before treating price or demand as validated. Record declines, objections and selection bias as well.
