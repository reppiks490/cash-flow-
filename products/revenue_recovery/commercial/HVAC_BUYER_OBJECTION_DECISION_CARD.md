## Objection-handling decision tree (rehearsal wording only)

| Buyer objection | Evidence-bounded response | Disqualifying condition |
|---|---|---|
| “Jobber already follows up on quotes.” | First inspect whether the incumbent automation already meets the workflow. Do not sell duplication; compare the missing review or reconciliation step. | Existing tool already closes the documented gap at lower cost. |
| “Housecall Pro has pipeline automation.” | Ask which paid tier/add-ons are enabled and what is not covered; avoid asserting a feature is absent. | No independently demonstrated gap. |
| “ServiceTitan/Hatch already uses AI.” | Position an operator-supervised audit only if it reveals a measurable cross-system exception; never claim superior conversion. | Buyer cannot establish a missing workflow or lawful integration rights. |
| “Why $700 monthly?” | Explain this is an unvalidated managed-service price hypothesis, not a published offer or ROI promise. Compare incremental contribution and measured staff time separately from our own operating costs. | No plausible buyer value or ability to pay; do not pressure. |
| “Can it automatically text my customers?” | No. Current demo generates unsent drafts. A real channel requires legal, consent, suppression, provider, security, and owner authorization. | Requests unattended messaging or consent bypass. |
| “Show me your recovered revenue.” | Only synthetic scenarios are available; authenticated customer receipts and attributable closed revenue are not established. | Buyer requires proof of results that does not exist. |
| “Can we connect my CRM now?” | Not to this prototype. Private tenancy, vendor authorization, retention controls, and QA release gate are outstanding. | Requires immediate production credentials or PII. |

Vendor capability claims must be rechecked against official sources and plan terms before any approved customer-facing use; `COMPETITOR_EVIDENCE.md` is a dated research snapshot.
