## Measurable pilot decision gates — not yet satisfied

1. **Technical:** independent QA confirms local synthetic end-to-end path, negative cases, tenant isolation, suppression, idempotency and human-approval enforcement on a combined exact commit; security owner approves release scope.
2. **Legal/operational:** signed pilot agreement defines controller/processor roles, authorized channels, data minimization, deletion, incident response, vendor permissions, support hours, cancellations and refunds; no fictional SLA.
3. **Commercial validation:** owner-approved, lawful discovery (not performed here) determines current CRM baseline, missing capability, support effort and willingness to pay. Record only authorized private evidence outside public GitHub.
4. **Attribution:** distinguish inquiry → qualified → draft → *confirmed sent* → *confirmed appointment* → *verified completed job* → *authenticated collection*; deduplicate by tenant/job/payment and account for refunds. No stage transition is inferred from a mock.
5. **Economics:** separately calculate seller net profit = collected subscription receipts − refunds − payment fees − hosting/model costs − support/onboarding allocation − acquisition/other attributable costs (with tax treatment explicitly defined). Compare with $2,500/month target only when period-complete, reconciled evidence exists. Buyer ROI is a different calculation and cannot be added to seller profit.
