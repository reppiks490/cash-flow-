## Proposed service description

Provider prepares a configurable lead/estimate follow-up review queue for approved, existing inbound HVAC inquiries and quotes; may generate evidence-based, reviewable draft suggestions and a weekly pipeline report. No autonomous customer outreach or customer contact occurs without explicitly enabled, compliant, audited workflows and individually appropriate approval.

Provider does **not** guarantee recovered revenue, appointment volume, closed jobs, response rates or savings. Provider does not replace emergency dispatch, pricing/quoting, regulatory advice, the operator's CRM of record, or the operator's responsibility for consumer requests.
