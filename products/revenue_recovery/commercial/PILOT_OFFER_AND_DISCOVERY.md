# HVAC Revenue Recovery — Pilot offer and discovery protocol

**Status:** unvalidated commercial hypothesis, synthetic-only demo. No actual paying customers, appointments, closed revenue, receipts, or conversion lift have been verified from a connected commercial source. **No outreach authorized.**

## Positioning and exclusions
For owner-operated HVAC service contractors with existing inbound inquiries, the proposed workflow helps operators review missed inquiries and outstanding quotes, prioritize follow-up, and prepare *unsent* drafts. It is not a lead-generation service, booking guarantee, autonomous texting service, or revenue guarantee. Do not describe any synthetic appointment or revenue as recovered.

## Proposed offer (subject to discovery)
- 30-day, owner-approved, no-live-data demonstration first. Proposed paid pilot only after explicit authorization, contracts, secure tenant isolation, provider permissions and release signoff.
- Hypothetical price: $700/month per business; optional setup fee remains $0 in the model and is not presumed earned.
- Prototype scope: synthetic intake, deduplication, evidence-backed qualification, operator-reviewed drafts, mock CRM preview, source-tagged funnel.
- Out of scope: outbound delivery, production CRM/email/calendar integration, unattended AI decisions, guaranteed bookings, handling sensitive customer data, invoicing or card charging.
- Service boundaries to negotiate: business-hours support window, incident reporting, retention/deletion, data ownership, uptime targets only after production observability. No invented SLA.
- Cancellation/refunds: must be specified in signed terms before payment activation; do not imply a live policy exists.

## Discovery questions (for future owner-approved conversations only)
1. What proportion of inbound calls/forms are unanswered, and how is this measured?
2. How are unsold estimates tracked today, and which CRM/calendar tools are used?
3. What permissions and suppression rules govern follow-ups by channel?
4. How many staff-hours per week are spent reviewing stalled inquiries?
5. What would make a draft trustworthy enough for an employee to approve?
6. How are bookings, completed jobs, refunds and paid receipts reconciled?
7. Who approves data access, messaging templates, retention and contracts?
8. Which failure modes (duplicate contact, opt-out, misattribution) are unacceptable?
9. What trial result would justify a subscription, excluding projected revenue?
10. Who controls the budget and what would trigger cancellation?

## Demo packet / operator script
1. Announce that every example is **synthetic** and no real provider is connected.
2. Load the three bundled HVAC demo inquiries in the same-origin local dashboard.
3. Show duplicate rejection and evidence/unknowns on qualification.
4. Inspect an unsent draft and mock CRM preview; confirm no send/booking occurs.
5. Show stage metrics with zero verified closed revenue and zero authenticated payments.
6. Record feedback as hypothetical until a signed pilot permits genuine measurement.

## Compliance / commercial gates
Human approval is necessary but not sufficient to contact anyone: independently verify lawful channel-specific consent, suppression status, applicable telemarketing/privacy requirements and vendor terms with counsel. Do not collect prospect details in public GitHub. Production requires authenticated tenants, private durable records, audit trail, data retention/deletion, integration review, and signed owner approval.
