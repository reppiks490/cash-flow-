A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Know what you’re buying.

Is this suitable for my agency’s clients?

It can be used as a white-label production pilot for an approved client project. You must have permission to use any logos, imagery, fonts, claims and other supplied assets.

What is outside the pilot?

Filming, voiceover, paid stock purchases, ad spend, publishing, account management, open-ended editing, native design-app formats and custom 3D production are separate from this fixed pilot.

Does this promise leads, sales or campaign performance?

No. Vellumflare does not promise leads, sales, revenue or campaign performance. You are buying defined creative deliverables; outcomes depend on the offer, audience, placement and follow-through.

How are delivery, cancellation and refunds handled?

The 48-hour production window begins only after accepted scope, confirmed payment, approved inputs and available capacity. Written terms are confirmed before payment; payment issues are resolved before accepting a replacement scope.

How is my information used here?

The brief composer below runs locally in your browser. Nothing is sent automatically. You choose whether to copy, download or open the prepared text in your own email app.
