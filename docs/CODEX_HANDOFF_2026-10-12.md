## 1. Current incumbent strategy

`economic/buying signal -> target company -> qualify -> identify reachable decision-maker -> create proof asset -> compliant outreach -> payment -> fulfillment -> attribution -> reinvest`

This currently outranks major zero-capital alternatives on speed-to-cash, control, probability of first revenue, margin, automation, and scalability.

Important: alternatives are not discarded. They are challengers or monetization rails inside the opportunity engine. These include:

- qualified lead / appointment brokerage

- digital products
- affiliate funnels
- owned media / newsletters
- creative production and repurposing

- data products
- performance-based services
- sponsorship / licensing / stock media where appropriate
- ecommerce / marketplace opportunities when genuinely zero-upfront
- longer-horizon IP/content businesses (including children's content) only when they do not displace faster cash paths

A challenger only replaces the incumbent if it wins on a rigorous score such as:

`Expected Value = P(first revenue) * speed * margin * addressable demand * automation * scalability * defensibility / capital requirement`

Use more dimensions when data is available: sales-cycle duration, buyer urgency, observable budget signal, decision-maker reachability, demonstrability, recurring revenue potential, platform dependence, compliance risk, and operational complexity.
