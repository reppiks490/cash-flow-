## Keep the credible parts

The narrow list is understandable and already producible: three portrait PNGs
and editable SVGs, a 15-second caption-led motion preview, three captions/CTA
options and one revision. PDFs visibly label the work uncommissioned, and no
client result, testimonial or revenue is claimed. The 48-hour clock properly waits
for agreed brief, payment and approved inputs. Private readbacks confirm the
existing drafts contain the reviewed PDF attachments and remain unsent.

## What weakens the pitch

"Useful for your current content workload" leaves the purchase trigger vague.
A large production agency may hear a generic creator pitch rather than a specific
piece of overflow work. A digital presence explanation is not itself evidence
that an established agency needs another supplier. Same-layout typography kits
also demonstrate a narrow visual style, not every target's actual client brand.

The motion preview is a caption-led sequence of graphics. It is not filming,
voiceover, complex video editing or demonstrated campaign performance. Current
rendering accepts text/colors; arbitrary logos, client photos, brand-font imports,
Canva/PSD/Figma native files and account publishing are not implemented features.
Do not accept a brief requiring those as if already included. SVG is editable,
but does not imply delivery in every agency's preferred software.

## One proposed controlled improvement

Keep the price, deliverables and existing specimen. Change only the final ask:

> If you have an overflow client brief this week, would a fixed $149 kit for one
> approved theme be useful? You can reply with the theme and intended audience.

This is original proposed copy, not a real conversation or source quotation.
It does not assume overflow exists or promise unscheduled availability. Confirm
actual capacity before accepting any deadline. The current 48-hour conditions,
commercial identification, valid mailing footer and stop instruction remain.

Acceptance criteria: the recipient can understand one concrete work unit, 149 USD,
required inputs, formats, exclusions and start conditions; no implied prior
relationship or outcomes. Operator/C05 must accept the controlled revision before
changing any live draft. Exact final body/hash and provider content must match.
Do not create A/B duplicates to the same company/contact; five sends cannot
produce a statistically reliable copy comparison.

## Intake and production checks

The existing private buyer-response kit already gathers audience, theme, CTA,
approved assets/style and acceptance. Before payment, confirm whether original
typography/SVG meets the client's needs. Resolve unfamiliar formats/assets or new
concepts as a separate agreed scope; do not sell first and discover incompatibility
later. Confirm asset permissions and approved factual claims. Legal rights and
client-specific font/asset licensing are not inferred from a public website.

One revision means one consolidated round within the accepted brief. Do not add
an unagreed new revision deadline, automatic refund rule or new ownership promise.
Those decisions are open and must be resolved with the accepted commercial scope.
Measure real human effort: briefing, copy/design, export, visual/video QA, revision
and delivery/access support. The price is not evidence of profitable labor.
