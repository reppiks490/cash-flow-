## What the evidence supports

Current private research contains 291 distinct captured domains: 169 saved homepage
texts, 91 same-domain search-excerpt cases and 31 unresolved domains. Excerpts are
weaker than full-page evidence. The provider-reported 9140 universe was not fully
exported. Homepage heuristics suggest 78 agency routes and 2 local routes; those
are candidates for review, not qualified buyers. Ten service-fit specimens and
existing unsent drafts have been verified. Zero sends, accepted orders or cash.

Purpose review found why keyword matching cannot approve a buyer: staffing
providers describe marketing roles; publishers write about agency services;
building-related vocabulary can describe tax engineering; a website agency can
mention trades clients; an agency may explicitly prohibit outsourcing; and a
white-label supplier is not automatically a purchaser of white-label work.
Seven target-level source decisions are retained privately. This does not assert
all remaining companies have passed review. No global opt-out or live packet was
altered; sourcing mismatch is a cohort exclusion, not a fabricated stop request.

## A frozen first test

- Offer: existing 149-dollar scope; one agreed theme, three PNG/SVG pairs, one
  15-second caption-led MP4, three captions/CTA options and one revision.
- Change proposed: one conditional concrete overflow-brief CTA; see offer review.
  Do not silently change the current drafts before C05/operator agreement.
- Cap: five confirmed initial sends only after identity/footer, user-owned live
  payment route, suppression, receipt integrity and operational ownership pass.
- Window: review actual replies daily while executing and assess this small cohort
  after several business days. The user extended the target on 2026-10-12 to profit
  within 14–20 days. Track net cash after recorded fees, refunds and costs; keep
  human effort and outstanding fulfillment obligations visible. Unknown costs
  prevent a verified profit claim. No background monitor is implied.
- Results: count actual sends, replies, stop requests, accepted scopes, paid orders,
  refunds, fees, approved assets, production and delivery separately.
- Evidence: an accepted paid scope supports demand for this narrow pilot. Five
  messages cannot establish a reliable conversion rate or superiority claim.
- Stop: opt-outs, conflicting/ambiguous provider outcomes or lost state immediately
  block relevant sends. No useful interest after the window triggers offer/cohort
  review, not automatic broad-scale outreach. No invoice is counted as cash.

The catalog's minimum three paid conversions and 15% relative-advantage promotion

## Unit economics: illustrations, not profit

At the existing 149-dollar price, Stripe's published domestic-card fee of 2.9%
plus 30 cents gives a rounded 4.62-dollar fee and 144.38 dollars before labor,
refunds, taxes, other costs and account-specific additions. Standard payments
has no setup/monthly charge. [Primary pricing](https://stripe.com/en-us/pricing).
Actual transaction/fee receipts replace this illustration when a buyer pays.

| Total human effort assumption | 144.38 dollars divided by effort |
| --- | --- |
| 1 hour | 144.38 dollars/hour |
| 2 hours | 72.19 dollars/hour |
| 4 hours | 36.10 dollars/hour |

These are scenarios. Research, copy/design revision, client communication and QA
time have not been measured for a real order. Ten rendered specimens demonstrate
the fixed-format export capability, not end-to-end profitable fulfillment.

A captured customer payment, a held processor balance, withdrawable funds and
cash received in the bank are different measures. Report each from actual
provider evidence. Stripe commonly schedules the first payout 7–14 days after
first live payment; bank timing can add delay. This alone can exceed the user's
former seven-day usable-cash target. The revised 14–20-day profit window still
requires actual payout eligibility, costs and completed obligations. [Primary payout policy](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts).
An existing verified suitable account may be faster, but actual holds/ownership
must be checked. Payment setup remains incomplete here; no instant payout or
access to another account is assumed.
