Research audit is the strongest adjacent test if a concrete issue can be shown.
It still needs agreement on scope/price before checkout, and technical changes
require customer authorization/access. This document does not set a new audit
price. Direct local creative can use the existing 149-dollar scope only when the
format/message actually fits; filming or regulatory advice is not included.
Digital-product pricing/license terms stay open; no contract is fabricated.

## Distribution and usable-cash screen

Compare distribution separately from the deliverable. A marketplace profile is
not demand, and payment collected is not necessarily available in the bank.
The user extended the target to profit within 14–20 days on 2026-10-12. This
gives payout clearance more time, but does not establish demand or profit. Count
fees, refunds and recorded fulfillment costs; keep unmeasured labor explicit.
Current primary policies were checked on 2026-10-12:

| Gumroad Discover | 30%, processing included; 104.30 remains | Marketplace discovery is not guaranteed distribution; payout conditions still apply |

These are rounded comparable-price arithmetic, not prices adopted for the new
challengers, profit forecasts, actual receipts or guaranteed current account fees.
Taxes, refunds, withdrawal/FX fees, optional products and human effort are excluded.
Read the account's offered contract and actual receipts before relying on net cash.

Daily/instant Gumroad payouts have eligibility requirements, including account
history; no eligibility is established here. Own connected PayPal sales can follow
a different crediting route, but processor holds still require inspection. New
accounts must not be treated as fast cash merely because a payment button exists.
Do not use friends/family transfers for customer services or redirect marketplace
clients off-platform to evade rules. No account/profile/application was submitted.

## Decision

Keep the incumbent for a controlled existing-packet test once gates are satisfied.
Prepare a specific research-audit challenger when a real public issue is found;
do not start marketplace/profile/catalog sprawl while sales and payout eligibility
are unknown; reassess timing against the revised 14–20-day profit target. Revisit after measured paid results, not a revised scenario score.
All commercial email routes keep the existing truthful identity, valid mailing
footer and opt-out requirements; [FTC primary guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).
